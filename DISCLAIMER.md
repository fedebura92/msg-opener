## English

**MSG Opener is provided "as is".** It is a free personal project with no
warranty of any kind: it may contain bugs, it may not always display an email
exactly like Outlook does, and support is not guaranteed.

Good to know:

- **Read-only.** The program opens and displays `.msg` files; it does not modify
  them. Still, always keep backups of important emails.
- **Attachments.** An email can carry dangerous files. MSG Opener asks for
  confirmation before opening executable attachments, but it is **not antivirus
  software**. Do not open attachments from senders you do not know. When you open
  an attachment, a temporary copy is saved in the Windows temporary folder.
- **Privacy.** The program is designed to process emails on your own PC and has no
